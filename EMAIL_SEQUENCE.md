## Implementation Notes

### Timing
- Adjust timing based on actual project milestones
- Space emails 3-7 days apart to avoid fatigue
- Add/remove emails based on sales velocity

### Personalization
- Use [First Name] throughout
- Reference their buyer type (investor/first-time/etc.) if collected
- Segment by price range interest
- Segment by buyer vs. broker

### Tracking
- Track email opens and clicks
- Remove from sequence if they book appointment
- Move to "hot leads" list if they click pricing links
- Tag by engagement level

### Platforms
These sequences work with:
- Mailchimp
- ActiveCampaign
- HubSpot
- ConvertKit
- Keap/Infusionsoft
- Salesforce
- And more

---

## A/B Testing Ideas

### Subject Lines:
- Test with/without emojis
- Test "Union Glen" vs. "Your Union Glen Registration"
- Test urgency vs. informational tones

### Send Times:
- Test morning (9 AM) vs. evening (7 PM)
- Test weekdays vs. weekends

### Content:
- Test long-form vs. short-form emails
- Test image-heavy vs. text-only
- Test different CTAs ("Book Now" vs. "Learn More")

---

## Success Metrics

Track these KPIs:
- Open rate target: 25%+
- Click rate target: 3%+
- Appointment booking rate: 5%+
- Unsubscribe rate: <0.5%

Optimize based on data.
